Roof Leak Water Damage Help in Portage

A roof leak rarely starts where the stain appears. Water enters at a raised shingle, a split in the flashing or a worn skylight seal, follows the rafters and decking downhill, and finally lands on the insulation. A ceiling stain tends to arrive late, well after the space overhead first got wet. We find the full wet area and dry it out.

Our trade is water damage restoration, and roofing is a separate one. We work on the interior, which means removing wet insulation, drying drywall and framing, dealing with mold and rebuilding the ceiling. If rain is still coming in, we can tarp the roof as a temporary cover, or work alongside the roofer you choose so the leak is fixed before we close anything up.

First Five Minutes

Before We Arrive

  1. Cut Power to Wet FixturesIf water is dripping through a light fixture, a ceiling fan or near an outlet, switch off that circuit at the breaker and leave the fixture alone.
  2. Catch the DripsSet buckets or pans under active drips and move furniture, rugs and electronics out of the way. Plastic or towels spread on the floor will keep it from soaking.
  3. Relieve a Ceiling BulgeA ceiling that sags with trapped water can be drained: stay off to the side and make a small opening at its lowest spot, with a bucket underneath.
  4. Stay Off the RoofClimbing on slick shingles or crawling around a dark attic is not worth the risk. Note from inside where the water is coming in and leave the rest to professionals.
  5. Document What You SeeBefore anything is moved or discarded, get video and photos of the attic, the ceiling and damaged items, and then phone us so drying can begin.
Common Causes

Why This Happens

  • Missing or broken shingles, often after high wind, with bare underlayment beneath.
  • Flashing that has rusted, lifted or pulled away around chimneys, vents and walls.
  • Rubber boots around plumbing vent pipes that split after years in the sun.
  • Popped or exposed roofing nails, each one a tiny opening for rain.
  • Skylight seals and curbs that dry out, crack and let rain run inside.
If It Is Left

What the Water Damages

Mold in the Attic

A dark, warm attic with damp wood and paper-faced drywall suits mold well. Within 24 to 48 hours it can start growing, then move across the bottom of the roof deck.

Lights and Wiring

Leak water heads for openings in the ceiling, such as recessed lights, fan boxes and junction boxes. Keep that circuit off until someone has inspected it, since damp wiring may corrode or short out.

Inside the Walls

From the attic, a leak can drop into a stud bay and soak the wall insulation and drywall from behind. Often the one clue is paint that blisters or trim that swells at the floor.

Insulation in the Attic

Wet insulation mats down, stops insulating and holds water against the ceiling drywall for days. Fiberglass can sometimes be dried. Removal is the usual answer for wet cellulose.

Insurance

Insurance and Roof Leak Water Damage

Interior damage from a sudden, accidental roof leak, like one caused by a fallen limb or wind-torn shingles, is often covered under a homeowners policy. Leaks blamed on age, wear or skipped maintenance are usually denied, and the roof repair itself may be handled differently from the interior. We document the damage with photos and moisture readings for your adjuster, but coverage depends on your policy, so read it or ask your agent.

Cost

What Affects the Cost

  • The amount of soaked insulation in the attic, and if it must come out.
  • The length of time the leak went unnoticed, plus whether mold has started.
  • The amount of ceiling and wall drywall that needs to be cut out.
  • How easy the attic is to reach, how high the ceilings are, and if the roof needs a tarp before work begins.

Our Process

What Happens After You Call

1

Trace the Leak Path

Using thermal imaging and moisture meters, our team checks wall cavities, ceilings and the attic and charts the whole route the water took, even where the room below shows nothing.

2

Tarp and Protect

An exposed roof gets covered with a temporary tarp, or we line things up with your roofing contractor. Floors and belongings below the wet ceiling are covered as well.

3

Tear Out and Dry

Unsalvageable drywall and insulation come out, and dehumidifiers and air movers go into the attic space and each room under it, with readings taken until the framing has dried.

4

Treat and Rebuild

An antimicrobial product goes on any open wood that needs it. Once the roof repair is done, we replace insulation, hang and finish new drywall, then prime and paint to match the ceiling.
